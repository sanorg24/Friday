## PRIMARY STRATEGY: Etsy Digital Products (Approved Cycle 29)

**Status:** READY TO EXECUTE - Awaiting owner engagement
**Capital Required:** $0.60 initial (3 listings), $6.20 total Week 1-2
**Time Required:** 3-4 hours initial setup, 1-2 hours/week maintenance
**Target:** First sale within 4 weeks of launch

### Why This Strategy

*Market Validation:*
- Etsy budgeting/finance category shows consistent buyer demand
- Digital products have proven success (templates, planners, printables)
- Price points $3-15 accessible to broad market
- Search volume data supports category viability

*Owner Constraints Fit:*
- Limited time availability (1 hr/weekday, 2 hrs/weekend)
- Weekend 1 launch possible with focused 3-4 hour session
- Ongoing maintenance fits within weekly time budget
- Automated delivery reduces operational burden

*Financial Sense:*
- Minimal startup capital ($0.60 for 3 listings)
- Free design tools (Canva free tier)
- Low risk ($6.20 total Week 1-2 deployment)
- High margin (90%+ after Etsy fees)
- Scalable with evidence of traction

### Execution Roadmap

**Weekend 1: Launch (3-4 hours)**
1. Create Etsy shop (free, 15 min)
2. Design 3 budget planner PDFs in Canva (2 hrs)
   - Monthly budget template
   - Debt payoff tracker
   - Savings goal planner
3. Write optimized listings (1 hr)
   - SEO-friendly titles
   - Benefit-focused descriptions
   - 13 strategic tags per listing
4. Publish listings ($0.60)
5. Send shop URL to council for review

**Week 2: Optimize & Test**
- Monitor traffic and favorites data
- Adjust listings based on engagement patterns
- Deploy $5 Etsy Ads test on best performer
- Add 2-3 product variations ($0.60)
- Target first sale

**Week 3-4: Scale Evidence**
- Analyze sales data (if any)
- Expand successful products
- Cut underperformers
- Increase ad spend proportional to ROAS
- Build email list if sales momentum

**Month 2+: Product Line**
- Expand into complementary categories
- Test higher price points ($10-15)
- Develop product bundles
- Build brand recognition
- Target recurring customer base
