### ✅ OPTION 1: Etsy Digital Products Store [COUNCIL TOP PICK - CYCLES 4-12]
**Status:** Fully researched, ready to build when owner circumstances change  
**Model:** Sell digital printables (planners, templates, wall art, checklists) with zero marginal cost  
**Startup Cost:** $40 total ($2 listings + $13 Canva + $15 Creative Fabrica + $10 assets)  
**Timeline to Revenue:** 14-45 days (some sellers report week 1)  
**Ongoing Time Required:** 1-2 hours/week after initial setup  
**Revenue Potential:** $100-1000/month by month 3-6  
**Kill Criteria:** <5 sales after 60 days with 20+ listings  
**Owner Pattern Fit:** Weekend burst model (16 hrs setup over 2 weekends, then 1 hr/week maintenance)  

**Why Council Chose This:**
- Fastest validation (customers exist NOW on Etsy marketplace)
- Truly passive (same digital file sells infinitely with zero additional cost)
- Lowest risk ($40 vs. potentially $0 return is acceptable loss)
- Proven model (thousands of sellers making $100-10k/month)
- Creative skill building (Canva, AI tools, design, SEO)
- Scales without owner time (more listings = more passive income potential)

**Full Implementation Plan:** See business_plan.md Option 2

### ✅ OPTION 2: Niche Affiliate SEO Site [EVALUATED CYCLE 4-12]
**Status:** Fully researched, ready to build when owner circumstances change  
**Model:** Content site targeting buyer-intent keywords, monetized via Amazon Associates + niche affiliate programs  
**Startup Cost:** $32-35 ($12-15 domain/hosting + $20 AI writing tool)  
**Timeline to Revenue:** 60-120 days (SEO takes time)  
**Ongoing Time Required:** 2-4 hours/week  
**Revenue Potential:** $50-500/month by month 6  
**Kill Criteria:** Zero organic traffic by day 90  
**Owner Pattern Fit:** Weekend burst model (14 hrs setup over 2 weekends, then 2 hrs/week content)  

**Why Council Considered This:**
- True passive income at scale (evergreen content works 24/7)
- Valuable skill building (SEO, content, WordPress)
- Low maintenance once traffic builds
- Multiple revenue streams possible (ads, affiliates, sponsorships)

**Why Not Top Pick:**
- Slower to revenue (60-120 days vs. 14-45 days for Etsy)
- Algorithm risk (Google updates can kill traffic overnight)
- More competitive landscape
- Lower margins (Amazon Associates pays 1-4%)

**Full Implementation Plan:** See business_plan.md Option 1

### ✅ OPTION 3: Faceless YouTube Channel [EVALUATED CYCLE 4-12]
**Status:** Fully researched, ready to build when owner circumstances change  
**Model:** Evergreen tutorial/listicle videos with AI voiceovers + stock footage, monetized via AdSense  
**Startup Cost:** $50 ($22 ElevenLabs + $13 Canva + $10 assets + $5 branding)  
**Timeline to Revenue:** 180-365 days (need 1000 subs + 4000 watch hours for monetization)  
**Ongoing Time Required:** 2-3 hours/week  
**Revenue Potential:** $100-2000/month AFTER monetization threshold  
**Kill Criteria:** <500 subs after 6 months of consistent uploads  
**Owner Pattern Fit:** Weekend burst model (18 hrs setup over 2 weekends, then 2 hrs/week content)  

**Why Council Considered This:**
- Highest revenue potential at scale
- Evergreen content (videos work 24/7 forever)
- Pre-monetization opportunities (sponsorships, affiliate links in descriptions)
- Valuable skill building (video, scripting, YouTube SEO)

**Why Not Top Pick:**
- VERY long to monetization (6-12 months minimum)
- High risk of never reaching threshold (many channels fail)
- Algorithm dependent
- Content grind (need consistent uploads)

**Full Implementation Plan:** See business_plan.md Option 3

### ❌ REJECTED: AI-Powered Etsy Listing Optimization Service [CYCLE 1-3]
**Status:** Rejected in Cycle 3 due to owner execution capacity mismatch  
**Model:** Offer Etsy sellers AI-optimized listing titles, tags, descriptions for $25-50/listing  
**Why Rejected:**
- Requires daily customer communication (sales, delivery, support)
- Service model doesn't match owner's revealed behavior (unable to execute even zero-cost research tasks)
- Owner has ~1 hr/weekday availability, insufficient for client work
- Pivot to passive models better suited to weekend burst work pattern

**Lesson Learned:** Service businesses require consistent daily engagement. At micro-budgets with limited owner time, passive models are better fit.

## DECISION FRAMEWORK APPLIED

All ideas evaluated against:
1. **Market Size:** Is there demonstrated demand?
2. **Competition:** Can we differentiate at micro-budget?
3. **Customer Pain:** Is the problem urgent/expensive enough?
4. **Ease of Selling:** Can we reach customers without huge ad spend?
5. **Price Point:** Can we charge enough to be profitable?
6. **Margin:** What % of revenue is profit?
7. **Scalability:** Can revenue grow without proportional time increase?
8. **Automation Potential:** Can this run passively?
9. **Startup Cost:** Does it fit $100 budget?
10. **Speed to First Dollar:** How fast can we validate?
11. **Owner Time Required:** Does it match owner's actual availability pattern (weekend bursts, not daily)?
12. **Skill Building:** Does it teach valuable transferable skills?

**Winner:** Etsy Digital Products scored highest across all criteria for owner's specific constraints.
