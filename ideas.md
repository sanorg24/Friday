## IDEA 2: Print-on-Demand T-Shirts (RUNNER-UP - ON HOLD)

**Status:** Alternative if Etsy digital products show poor traction

**Concept:** Design t-shirts, use Printful/Printify integration with Etsy for fulfillment.

**Pros:**
- Similar market to digital products
- No inventory risk (printed on demand)
- Higher price point ($15-25 per shirt)
- Etsy integration automated

**Cons:**
- Lower margins (40-50% vs 95%+ for digital)
- Longer feedback loop (production + shipping time)
- More customer service (sizing, shipping issues)
- Slightly higher startup cost (mockup tools)

**When to Consider:**
- If digital products show views but no sales after 3 weeks
- If physical product demand signals emerge
- If specific niche shows strong engagement but digital format limiting
