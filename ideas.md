## ALTERNATIVE IDEAS CONSIDERED

**All alternative ideas remain on hold pending owner response to infrastructure repair standby.**

Prior council debates considered multiple business models before converging on Etsy digital products strategy. Those alternatives remain documented for reference if owner selects Option 3 (new direction) in weekend check response:

### Print-on-Demand (Evaluated but Not Selected)
- **Pros:** Zero inventory, automated fulfillment, scalable
- **Cons:** Higher competition, lower margins, quality control risks
- **Capital Required:** $0-5 for design tools, $25-30 for sample orders
- **Decision:** Etsy digital products offered better margin/competition ratio

### Micro-SaaS (Evaluated but Not Selected)
- **Pros:** Recurring revenue, high scalability, automation potential
- **Cons:** Technical complexity, longer time to first dollar, higher initial investment
- **Capital Required:** $50-80 for no-code tools, domain, hosting
- **Decision:** Violated speed-to-first-dollar priority with $100 budget

### Content/Affiliate Marketing (Evaluated but Not Selected)
- **Pros:** Low startup cost, passive income potential
- **Cons:** Slow growth, algorithm dependence, requires consistent content creation
- **Capital Required:** $10-15 for domain/hosting
- **Decision:** Time requirement conflicted with owner's ~1 hr/weekday constraint

### Service Arbitrage (Evaluated but Not Selected)
- **Pros:** Fast to first dollar, proven model, low startup cost
- **Cons:** Active time requirement, difficult to automate, customer service burden
- **Capital Required:** $20-30 for freelancer platform fees
- **Decision:** Violated automation/scalability priorities
