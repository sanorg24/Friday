## EVALUATION FRAMEWORK

Ideas scored on:
- **Market Size:** Addressable customer base
- **Competition:** Ease of differentiation
- **Customer Pain:** Severity of problem solved
- **Ease of Selling:** Sales complexity
- **Price Point:** Revenue per transaction
- **Margin:** Profit after costs
- **Scalability:** Growth without proportional time increase
- **Automation Potential:** Can it run without owner?
- **Startup Cost:** Capital required to launch
- **Speed to First Dollar:** Time until first revenue
- **Owner Time Required:** Hours per week to maintain

**Etsy Digital Products scored highest across all dimensions given our constraints:**
- $100 budget
- Owner has ~1 hr/weekday, ~2 hrs/weekend
- Need revenue within weeks, not months
- Must be automatable
- Owner has adjacent experience
