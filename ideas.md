## ALTERNATIVE STRATEGIES (If Owner Pivots)

These remain viable if owner's circumstances or preferences change:

### Print-on-Demand (Printful + Etsy)
- Zero inventory risk
- Higher price points ($15-30/item)
- Requires stronger design/marketing
- 2-3 week customer delivery time

### Micro-SaaS Tool
- Higher income potential ($29-99/mo)
- Requires technical skills or no-code tools
- Longer time to first revenue (6-12 weeks)
- Better long-term scalability

### Local Service Arbitrage
- Fast cash flow (Week 1 possible)
- Requires active customer acquisition
- Not passive/automated
- Geographic limitations

### Content Monetization
- Low startup cost
- Long runway to revenue (3-6 months)
- Platform algorithm risk
- High competition

---

**Decision Framework:**

All alternatives evaluated through Money Filter: market size, competition, customer pain, ease of selling, price point, margin, scalability, automation potential, startup cost, speed to first dollar, owner time required. Current Etsy strategy scores highest across weighted criteria for $100 budget + owner's time constraints.
