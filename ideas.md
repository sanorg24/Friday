# ARCHITECT Business Ideas - Evaluated Options

## CURRENT PLAN: Etsy Digital Products (Approved Cycle 29)

**Status:** Ready to execute, awaiting owner engagement to begin Weekend 1 checklist

**Concept:** Sell budget planner PDFs and financial organization templates on Etsy marketplace.

**Money Filter Analysis:**
- Market size: Large (Etsy budgeting category active)
- Competition: Moderate (differentiation through quality/bundling)
- Customer pain: High (people struggle with budget tracking)
- Ease of selling: Medium (Etsy handles discovery, we handle optimization)
- Price point: $3-8 per product (sweet spot for impulse digital purchases)
- Margin: 95%+ (digital product, automated delivery)
- Scalability: High (create once, sell infinitely)
- Automation potential: High (Etsy auto-delivers, we optimize listings)
- Startup cost: $0.60-6.20 (listing fees + optional ads)
- Speed to first dollar: 1-4 weeks realistic
- Owner time: 3-4 hrs setup, 1-2 hrs/week maintenance

**Why This Won:** Best balance of low capital requirement, fast feedback loops, automation potential, and realistic first-revenue timeline for owner's time constraints.

**Execution Roadmap:**
- Weekend 1: Shop setup, 3 products designed/listed ($0.60)
- Week 2: Optimize based on traffic data, deploy $5 ad test
- Week 3-4: Add variations, target first sale
- Month 2+: Scale successful products, expand product line

## ALTERNATIVE IDEAS CONSIDERED

### Runner-Up: Micro-SaaS Chrome Extension

**Concept:** Productivity tool solving specific workflow pain point.

**Pros:**
- Recurring revenue potential (subscriptions)
- High automation once built
- Scalable to thousands of users

**Cons:**
- Requires development time (10-20 hrs initial build)
- Longer time to first dollar (4-8 weeks minimum)
- Higher complexity for $100 budget validation
- Owner's time constraints (1 hr/weekday) challenging for development

**Council Decision:** Strong long-term potential, but Etsy path offers faster validation with less upfront time investment. Could revisit if Etsy generates capital for development budget.

---

### Alternative: Print-on-Demand T-Shirts

**Concept:** Design-focused apparel sold through Printful/Printify + Etsy.

**Pros:**
- Zero inventory risk (print on demand)
- Automated fulfillment
- Etsy marketplace built-in

**Cons:**
- Lower margins (40-50% vs 95%+ digital)
- Higher competition in apparel space
- Shipping times affect customer experience
- Design differentiation harder without brand

**Council Decision:** Similar platform benefits to digital products but worse margins and harder differentiation. Digital products win on economics.

---

### Alternative: Freelance Services (Fiverr/Upwork)

**Concept:** Sell specific skills (writing, design, data analysis) on gig platforms.

**Pros:**
- Fast to first dollar (1-2 weeks possible)
- Low startup cost ($0-20 for premium profiles)
- Direct customer feedback

**Cons:**
- Time-for-money model (not scalable)
- Owner's limited hours (1 hr/weekday) constrains income
- Doesn't build asset that generates passive revenue
- Platform competition intense

**Council Decision:** Trades time for money instead of building scalable asset. Doesn't align with mission to create recurring income business.

---

### Alternative: Affiliate Niche Website

**Concept:** Content site targeting specific product category, monetized via Amazon Associates or niche affiliate programs.

**Pros:**
- Passive income potential once ranked
- Low ongoing maintenance
- Scalable content model

**Cons:**
- 3-6 months minimum to SEO traction
- Requires consistent content creation (time intensive)
- Algorithm risk (Google updates can tank traffic)
- Longer capital recovery timeline

**Council Decision:** Too slow for initial $100 validation. Could be Month 6+ expansion if Etsy generates content creation budget.

---

### Alternative: Notion Templates

**Concept:** Sell productivity templates on Gumroad or Notion template marketplaces.

**Pros:**
- Similar to Etsy digital products model
- High margins, automated delivery
- Growing Notion user base

**Cons:**
- Smaller total market than Etsy
- Less built-in discovery (Gumroad requires external traffic)
- Template marketplace saturation increasing

**Council Decision:** Very close second to Etsy. Etsy won due to larger marketplace and better built-in discovery. Could run parallel if Etsy validates digital product demand.

## EVALUATION FRAMEWORK

All ideas evaluated against Money Filter:
1. Market size (TAM)
2. Competition intensity
3. Customer pain severity
4. Ease of selling
5. Price point economics
6. Margin structure
7. Scalability potential
8. Automation potential
9. Startup cost
10. Speed to first dollar
11. Owner time required (constrained to 1 hr/weekday, 2 hrs/weekend)

**Winner Selection Criteria:**
- Fastest path to validation with $100 budget
- Lowest time requirement for owner's constraints
- Highest probability of first dollar within 4 weeks
- Best foundation for scaling if successful
