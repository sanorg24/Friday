## ALTERNATIVE IDEAS CONSIDERED

### Content Arbitrage (Rejected - Higher Risk)
- Buy PLR content, repackage, sell on marketplaces
- **Pros:** Low startup cost, proven model
- **Cons:** Quality control challenges, market saturation, requires upfront content purchase
- **Why Etsy Better:** Etsy allows original creation with zero upfront content costs

### Print-on-Demand (Rejected - Lower Margins)
- Design uploaded to Printful/Printify, sold via Etsy
- **Pros:** Zero inventory, automated fulfillment
- **Cons:** 20-40% margins vs 95%+ for digital, slower fulfillment feedback
- **Why Etsy Digital Better:** Higher margins, instant delivery, faster learning cycles

### Micro-SaaS (Rejected - Time Constraints)
- Small software solving specific problem
- **Pros:** Recurring revenue potential, high scalability
- **Cons:** Development time exceeds owner availability, requires ongoing maintenance
- **Why Etsy Better:** Matches 1hr/weekday constraint, no technical maintenance

### Affiliate Marketing (Rejected - Slower Feedback)
- Content creation driving affiliate commissions
- **Pros:** Zero product creation, unlimited scale
- **Cons:** 60-90 days to meaningful traffic/revenue data, algorithm-dependent
- **Why Etsy Better:** Faster feedback loop, direct customer relationship

## EVALUATION FRAMEWORK

All ideas evaluated against:
1. **Market size:** Addressable audience and demand
2. **Competition:** Market saturation and differentiation
3. **Customer pain:** Severity of problem being solved
4. **Ease of selling:** Sales friction and conversion barriers
5. **Price point:** Revenue per transaction
6. **Margin:** Profit after costs
7. **Scalability:** Growth potential without linear cost increase
8. **Automation potential:** Reduction of owner time per transaction
9. **Startup cost:** Capital required to first dollar
10. **Speed to first dollar:** Time until revenue validation
11. **Owner time required:** Match with 1hr weekday / 2hr weekend availability
