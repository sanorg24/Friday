## CURRENT STRATEGY: Etsy Digital Products (Approved Cycle 29)

**Status:** Ready to execute immediately upon owner engagement

**Core Concept:**
Create and sell digital budget planner templates on Etsy targeting individuals seeking financial organization tools.

**Why This Idea:**
- Validated market demand (Etsy budgeting category has active buyers)
- Minimal startup cost ($0.20/listing, free design tools)
- Fits owner's time constraints (3-4 hrs initial setup, 1-2 hrs/week maintenance)
- Fast feedback loops (listings live within hours)
- Automated delivery (Etsy handles fulfillment)
- Scalable (proven templates can expand to product line)

**Execution Roadmap:**
1. Weekend 1: Create shop, design 3 products, publish listings ($0.60)
2. Week 2: Optimize based on traffic data, test $5 Etsy Ads
3. Week 3-4: Add product variations, target first sale
4. Month 2+: Scale investment proportional to traction evidence

**Money Filter Score: 8.5/10**
- Market size: Large (millions of Etsy buyers)
- Competition: Moderate (can differentiate through design/features)
- Customer pain: High (financial disorganization is common frustration)
- Ease of selling: High (Etsy handles discovery and transactions)
- Price point: $3-8 per product (sweet spot for impulse purchases)
- Margin: ~90% after Etsy fees
- Scalability: Excellent (digital products, unlimited inventory)
- Automation potential: High (automated delivery, passive income after setup)
- Startup cost: Minimal ($0.60 listings, $5-10 ads testing)
- Speed to first dollar: 2-4 weeks realistic
- Owner time required: 3-4 hrs setup, 1-2 hrs/week maintenance (fits constraints)
