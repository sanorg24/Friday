**Market Validation:**

- Digital products trending on Etsy (low competition vs physical goods)
- Price points: $3-15 per product, 40-60% margin after Etsy fees
- Customer pain: overwhelmed brides need structured planning tools

**First Three Products ($0.60 total cost):**
1. Ultimate Wedding Planning Checklist ($0.20 Canva template)
2. Wedding Budget Tracker Spreadsheet ($0.20 Google Sheets template)
3. Wedding Timeline Template ($0.20 Canva timeline)

**Capital Efficiency:**
- Shop setup: $0 (Etsy free to open)
- First listing: $0.20 (Etsy listing fee)
- Product creation: Canva free tier, Google Sheets free
- Total first-product cost: $0.20
- Remaining budget for scaling: $99.80

**Time Alignment:**
- Product creation: 2-3 hours per template (weekend work)
- Shop management: 15-30 min/day (weekday maintenance)
- Customer service: As needed (digital = minimal)
- Fits within owner's ~1 hr/weekday, ~2 hrs/weekend constraint

**Revenue Model:**
- Product price: $4.99-$9.99 per digital download
- Etsy fees: ~$0.20 listing + 6.5% transaction + 3% payment processing
- Net per sale: ~$4.00-$8.00 (60-80% margin)
- Break-even: 1 sale of lowest-priced product
- Profit threshold: Every sale after first

**Scalability Path:**
1. Launch 3 core products (Week 1)
2. Gather customer feedback (Week 2-3)
3. Expand product line based on data (Week 4+)
4. Build email list for repeat customers
5. Create product bundles for higher AOV
6. Automate delivery and customer communication

**Automation Potential:**
- Product delivery: Automatic via Etsy
- Customer emails: Etsy automated messages
- Marketing: Pinterest pins (schedule weekly)
- Analytics: Etsy built-in dashboard
- Minimal ongoing manual work after setup

**Risk Mitigation:**
- Low capital risk ($0.60 for first three products)
- Fast feedback loop (know within 2-3 weeks if viable)
- Easy pivot (digital products = no sunk inventory costs)
- Market-tested niche (existing demand proven)

**Success Metrics:**
- Week 1: Shop live, 3 products listed
- Week 2: First sale (validation)
- Week 3: 5+ sales or pivot decision

## EVALUATION FRAMEWORK

All ideas evaluated against:
1. **Market Size:** Addressable customer base
2. **Competition:** Barrier to entry and saturation
3. **Customer Pain:** Severity of problem being solved
4. **Ease of Selling:** Sales cycle and conversion complexity
5. **Price Point:** Revenue per transaction
6. **Margin:** Profit after all costs
7. **Scalability:** Growth potential without linear effort increase
8. **Automation:** Ability to reduce owner time as business grows
9. **Startup Cost:** Capital required to launch (must fit $100 budget)
10. **Speed to First Dollar:** Time until first revenue
11. **Owner Time Required:** Alignment with ~1 hr/weekday, ~2 hrs/weekend
